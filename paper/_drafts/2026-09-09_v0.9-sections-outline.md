¶ D-2  CLAIM: boundary_screen. an observed statistic at exactly 0 or exactly 1
       of its attainable range cannot converge under method of moments, and this
       is checkable before fitting
  move: warrant
  - R/searchnet-screens.R:138-144
      "Under method-of-moments estimation an effect whose observed statistic
      lies exactly on the boundary of its attainable support cannot converge:
      the estimate diverges, because no parameter value makes the simulated mean
      equal a value the simulation can only approach. Membership is a property
      of the observed data and of the sampling rule that produced it, so it is
      checkable before any model is fitted, and this function is the whole
      check."  [VERIFIED - read]
  - R/searchnet-screens.R:146-151
      "A balanced panel requires every actor to hold a tie in every wave, which
      puts the `outIso` statistic at exactly zero and `outTrunc(1)` and
      `outThreshold(1)` at exactly their maxima. The sampling rule, not the
      dataset, is what decides."  [VERIFIED - read]
  - the example in the roxygen is the demonstration: same array, unbalanced then
      balanced, opposite verdicts on `outIso`
      [VERIFIED - read R/searchnet-screens.R:172-180]
  GAP: "exactly 0 or exactly 1" is implemented through `all.equal` on a computed
       position (R/searchnet-screens.R:64-73). Floating-point exactness is a
       tolerance, not an identity. Nothing states the tolerance.
  YOUR CALL: does the paper claim the check is exact, or that it is exact to
       `all.equal` default tolerance. The second is what the code does.
```

```
¶ D-3  CLAIM: scope_confound_screen. row-normalization does NOT fix the
       dense-coupling confound; banding does
  move: contrast; the reflex remedy is the one that fails
  - R/searchnet-screens.R:281-288
      "Three treatments of W are reported side by side, because the reflex
      remedy is the one that does not work. \strong{Row-normalization does not
      touch the confound}: it rescales the statistic but leaves the density
      pattern that produced it, so its correlation is effectively
      indistinguishable from the raw matrix and its nonzero share is identical.
      \strong{Banding does}: keeping only the strongest tail sparsifies W, and
      the statistic stops tracking portfolio size."  [VERIFIED - read]
  - the mechanism, R/searchnet-screens.R:272-276
      "When W is dense that sum is approximately the mean coupling times actor

      variance instead of carrying pairwise structure. The symptom during

      effect collapses."  [VERIFIED - read]
  - `threshold` default 0.5, and its roxygen says "This is a reporting

      [VERIFIED - read R/searchnet-screens.R:300-302]
  THEREFORE: this screen bears directly on the paper's own XWX effect, which
      §3.3 and §3.4 present without any note that a dense W re-measures scope
  GAP: the paper's worked example (§4.2, Step 2) uses a block-diagonal W of 4

  YOUR CALL: run the screen on the paper's own running example and report the
